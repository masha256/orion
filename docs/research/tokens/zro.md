# LayerZero (ZRO)

Researched 2026-09-22. Rank #47 by market cap (layer-1 tokens, stablecoins and tokenized RWAs excluded). Market cap $0.49B, FDV $1.37B, price $1.38 (CoinGecko, 2026-09-22).
Chain(s): Ethereum (canonical `0x6985884c4392d348587b19cb9eaaf157f13271cd`, the same address as an OFT on Base, Arbitrum, BNB Chain, Optimism, Polygon, Avalanche). CoinGecko id: `layerzero`. DefiLlama slugs: `layerzero-v2`, `layerzero-v1` (parent `layerzero`), `stargate-v2`.

## Verdict

Orion fit: B. Messaging fees ($1.9M in the last year) go entirely to DVNs and executors; the protocol fee switch is still at 0%. The only holder flow is a discretionary ZRO buy-and-hold funded by Stargate bridge revenue, which the Foundation took over in the Aug 2025 STG acquisition. DefiLlama tracks it as holders revenue ($3.39M over 1y), and it can be read on Ethereum as ZRO inflows to the OneSig buyback wallet. The buyback is discretionary and held rather than burned, and monthly unlocks outpace it.

- A: revenue-funded holder flow exists, is measurable (on-chain or DefiLlama), and revenue is published. Config-only or near config-only in Orion.
- B: real revenue and a holder flow exist but something is hard: off-chain data, discretionary capture, non-EVM chain needing an adapter, or an equity layer that keeps most value.
- C: revenue exists but nothing reaches holders today (fee switch off, all to validators/treasury), or the flow is inflation-funded; a valuation would rest entirely on an assumed future capture.
- D: no revenue model (store of value, meme, pure inflation staking). Not suitable.

## Business and revenue

- LayerZero messaging: users pay native-token fees per cross-chain message to DVNs (verifiers) and executors. DefiLlama `layerzero-v2`: fees 30d $247,351, 1y $1,907,322. "LayerZero takes a 0% protocol take rate on messaging fees"; about 100% goes to supply side. V1: fees 1y $331k, protocol revenue 0. The protocol fee switch (a fee on messages, paid to ZRO) has not been activated as of 2026-09-22 per DefiLlama's methodology. History of the semi-annual fee-switch votes: unconfirmed.
- Stargate (bridge): acquired by the LayerZero Foundation through a StargateDAO vote that closed 2025-08-16 (For 7.22M vs Against 0.40M veSTG). Terms: every STG swapped for 0.08634 ZRO; veSTG holders got 50% of Stargate top-line revenue for six months, the other 50% went to ZRO buybacks; after six months (about Feb 2026) "all future excess revenue generated by Stargate will be directed towards reducing the circulating supply of ZRO via a buyback program." STG-to-ZRO conversion closes 2026-12-15 (LayerZero ecosystem update, Sept 2026). DefiLlama `stargate-v2`: revenue 30d $315,275 (≈ $3.8M annualized), 1y $1,139,564.
- LayerZero Labs is a private company; the Foundation holds and operates Stargate.

## Holder flows

- id `zro_stargate_buyback`, kind `buy_and_hold` (bought-back ZRO accumulates in a OneSig wallet; not burned, though the proposal says "reducing the circulating supply"), capture_rule `discretionary` (Foundation-operated; the rule comes from a dissolved DAO's proposal, with no contract enforcing it), recipient_base `all`.
- Trailing annualized: $3,391,193 (DefiLlama `layerzero-v2` holders revenue, 1y); 30d $191,090 (≈ $2.3M annualized). Monthly: Nov 2025 $1.19M, Jan 2026 $475k, Feb $375k, Mar $286k, Apr $264k, May $207k, Jun $141k, Jul $135k, Aug $132k, Sep $191k. The Nov 2025 lump is probably accumulated early buybacks.
- Observability: ZRO received by the buyback wallet `0x6ac55e733dff03a54251670df0667774e8f7d28f` (Etherscan: OneSig contract, holding 2,375,473 ZRO ≈ $3.3M on 2026-09-22). EVM `transfer_flow` on Ethereum can read it.
- Changes: buybacks started after the Aug 2025 acquisition. The 50% veSTG revenue share ended about Feb 2026. No announced burn of the accumulated ZRO.

Capture rate today: holder flow / revenue. Against Stargate revenue (the funding source) the buyback of about $2.3M annualized (30d) vs revenue of about $3.8M annualized ≈ 60%, and 1y $3.39M vs $1.14M (>100%, timing and early lumps). Against messaging fees, capture is 0%. Recommend revenue = Stargate revenue + protocol messaging revenue (0 today).

## Supply

Total/max 1,000,000,000 ZRO; circulating 353,313,326 (35.3%, CoinGecko); FDV/mcap 2.8x. Allocation (Tokenomist): community 38.3%, strategic partners 32.2%, core contributors 25.5%, tokens repurchased 4.0%. Unlocks: monthly releases to strategic partners and core contributors on the 20th; next 2026-10-20; the schedule runs into 2027 (Tokenomist). Size about 25.7M ZRO/month (≈ $35M/month, about 10x the buyback rate) until about June 2027 (size and end date from memory, unconfirmed). Community allocation releases are discretionary. No emissions, no staking yield, no burns. supply_basis: `circulating` plus the unlock step schedule (or `effective_total` of 1B for conservatism); the buyback wallet balance could be treated as non-circulating.

## Orion mapping

- Modules: `holder_cashflow` 0.4, `forward_multiple` on revenue 0.3 (Stargate + messaging protocol revenue), `forward_multiple` on holder_flow 0.3.
